# Procedures

Read the selected procedure and its page.md before acting. Each procedure includes its widget documentation and full input/output schemas.

- [activity.posts](activity/procedures/posts.md) — The posts on the activity page, newest first, scrolling the page until there are as many as asked or it ends: each with its address, kind, author, age, text as shown and whether it is cut short, and its counts of reactions, comments and reposts. Reads only.
- [connections.list](connections/procedures/list.md) — The connections on the page, newest first, scrolling until there are as many as asked or it shows no more: each with their name, profile address, headline and the date the connection was made. Reads only.
- [invitations.sent](invitations/procedures/sent.md) — The pending invitations the signed-in member sent, newest first, scrolling until there are as many as asked or the page shows no more: each with the name, profile address, headline, when it was sent and the note if one was sent. Reads only: Withdraw is never clicked.
- [messaging.conversations](messaging/procedures/conversations.md) — The conversations in the list, newest first, loading more until there are as many as asked or it shows no more: each with its name, participants, the time of the last message, that message as a preview and who sent it, whether it is open and whether it is unread. Reads only.
- [messaging.select](messaging/procedures/select.md) — Open a conversation on the right: by its name as the list shows it, loading more of the list until it is found, or by its address, /messaging/thread/<id>/. LinkedIn marks the conversation read when it opens, as it does for anyone opening it; nothing is typed or sent. Ends on messaging, with that conversation open.
- [messaging.thread](messaging/procedures/thread.md) — The open conversation: who it is with — name, headline, profile address — or the group it is, and its messages as the pane shows them, oldest first: who sent each, whether it was you, when, and the text. The latest messages: older ones the pane has not loaded are not read. Reads only.
- [post.reactors](post/procedures/reactors.md) — Who reacted to the post, each as a basic profile: opens the Reactions dialog, reads its list — scrolling it until it ends or there are as many as asked — and closes it again. A member with their name, profile address, reaction, degree and headline; a company page with its name, address and reaction. Reads only.
- [post.read](post/procedures/read.md) — The post as its page shows it: the author, the group it was posted in if any, the age, the text in full, the media, and the counts of reactions, comments and reposts. Reads only.
- [profile.connect](profile/procedures/connect.md) — Send a connection invitation to the member whose profile is open, with the note given or without one. The one method here that writes: it clicks Send, once, only when told to by this call. Verifies the top card shows Pending after. Never withdraws, never retries a Send.
- [profile.detail](profile/procedures/detail.md) — The member's profile in detail: the top card — name, headline, location, degree of connection, connections and followers as shown, company and school, the profile's address — and the About text, whole. Reads only.
- [profile.education](profile/procedures/education.md) — The member's Education card: every school it shows — the school and its page, the degree and the dates as shown — and whether a Show all link leads to more. Reads only.
- [profile.experience](profile/procedures/experience.md) — The member's Experience card: every position it shows — title, company and its page, employment type, dates, duration, location, description as shown — and whether a Show all link leads to more. Reads only.
- [profile.posts](profile/procedures/posts.md) — The posts the profile's Activity card shows — the member's latest, a preview of their activity page: each with its address, kind, author, age, text as shown and whether it is cut short, and its counts of reactions, comments and reposts. Reads only.
