# connections

the signed-in member's connections: a heading with their count, a search box, then one entry per connection, newest first — name, headline, the date it was made

Path pattern: `^/mynetwork/invite-connect/connections/?`

Open: `https://www.linkedin.com/mynetwork/invite-connect/connections/`. Replace the placeholder with the member's slug or post URN.

The heading `<n> connections` at the top of the main column, the `Search your connections` box under it, then the entries, each with the member's name and headline and `Connected on <date>` under them.
