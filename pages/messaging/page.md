# messaging

the signed-in member's messaging: the conversation list on the left — one entry per conversation, newest first, with the last message as a preview — and the open conversation on the right, its messages newest last

Path pattern: `^/messaging(?:/|$)`

Open: `https://www.linkedin.com/messaging/`. Replace the placeholder with the member's slug or post URN.

The heading `Messaging` with the `Search messages` box at the top, the list `Conversation List` on the left, and on the right the open conversation: its member as a heading, then its messages, then the `Write a message…` box.
